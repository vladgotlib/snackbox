How to play

flip it done avoid
  • Sweep the pointer over every pale tile to flip it.
  • Keep moving. A tile is only safe while it spins — still on it when it lands costs a penalty.
  • Touching a black tile costs a penalty too.
  • Your score is seconds elapsed + 3 per penalty, across all 10 levels. Lowest wins.
