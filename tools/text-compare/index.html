Text Compare

Paste two versions of your writing and see exactly what changed.

Private: nothing leaves this page

Runs 100% in your browser. There is no server. The comparison happens on your own computer.

A built-in security policy blocks every network connection from this page, so the text physically cannot be sent anywhere, even by accident.

Nothing is saved. Closing or reloading the tab erases everything.

Browser spellcheck and grammar extensions (like Grammarly) are switched off in the text boxes, since some of them send text to the cloud.
